## 1.5 Accessibility Contrast Ratios (WCAG 2.1 AA)

| Foreground | Background | Ratio | Normal Text (4.5:1) | Large Text (3:1) |
|------------|------------|-------|---------------------|------------------|
| `#5B2C6F` | `#FFFFFF` | **7.2:1** | ✅ Pass | ✅ Pass |
| `#5B2C6F` | `#F5F5F5` | **6.8:1** | ✅ Pass | ✅ Pass |
| `#5B2C6F` | `#FAFAFA` | **6.6:1** | ✅ Pass | ✅ Pass |
| `#2E7D32` | `#FFFFFF` | **5.9:1** | ✅ Pass | ✅ Pass |
| `#2E7D32` | `#F5F5F5` | **5.6:1** | ✅ Pass | ✅ Pass |
| `#E65100` | `#FFFFFF` | **4.6:1** | ✅ Pass | ✅ Pass |
| `#E65100` | `#F5F5F5` | **4.3:1** | ❌ Fail (use large only) | ✅ Pass |
| `#FFFFFF` | `#5B2C6F` | **7.2:1** | ✅ Pass | ✅ Pass |
| `#FFFFFF` | `#2E7D32` | **5.9:1** | ✅ Pass | ✅ Pass |
| `#FFFFFF` | `#E65100` | **4.6:1** | ✅ Pass | ✅ Pass |
| `#212121` | `#FFFFFF` | **16.1:1** | ✅ Pass | ✅ Pass |
| `#757575` | `#FFFFFF` | **4.6:1** | ✅ Pass | ✅ Pass |
| `#9E9E9E` | `#FFFFFF` | **2.8:1** | ❌ Fail | ❌ Fail |

### Accessibility Rules
1. **Never use Orange (`#E65100`) as text on light backgrounds below 24px** — contrast drops below 4.5:1
2. **Always use `#757575` or darker for body text** on white backgrounds
3. **Minimum contrast for interactive elements:** 3:1 (buttons, links, form controls)
4. **Focus indicators:** Must have 3:1 contrast against adjacent colors
5. **Do not use color alone** to convey information — supplement with icons or text

# 3. VISUAL LANGUAGE

## 3.1 Photography Style Guidelines

### Editorial Photography
- **Style:** Documentary-journalistic with editorial polish
- **Lighting:** Natural light preferred; golden hour for feature stories
- **Composition:** Rule of thirds; leading lines; environmental context
- **Color Treatment:** Warm tones that complement the brand palette; avoid heavy filters
- **Subjects:** People in authentic contexts — working, celebrating, creating

### Portrait Photography
- **Style:** Environmental portraits (subject in their element)
- **Framing:** Medium close-up to full-body depending on context
- **Background:** Meaningful context (office, workshop, landscape)
- **Expression:** Genuine, confident, approachable — never staged or artificial
- **Technical:** Sharp focus on eyes; shallow depth of field for separation

### Landscape/Location Photography
- **Style:** Wide establishing shots that showcase Plateau's natural beauty
- **Time of Day:** Golden hour (early morning/late afternoon) preferred
- **Weather:** Embrace dramatic skies, mist, and atmospheric conditions
- **Composition:** Foreground interest; leading lines; scale references
- **Post-Processing:** Vibrant but natural; enhance rather than transform

### Photography Do's
✅ Capture authentic moments and genuine emotions  
✅ Show diversity of Plateau's people, cultures, and landscapes  
✅ Maintain consistent warm color grading across all images  
✅ Include environmental context that tells a story  
✅ Use high-resolution images (minimum 2400px wide for hero images)  
✅ Credit photographers prominently  

### Photography Don'ts
❌ Use stock photos that feel generic or staged  
❌ Apply heavy filters that distort natural colors  
❌ Crop people awkwardly or cut off important elements  
❌ Use low-resolution or blurry images  
❌ Show subjects in unflattering or undignified contexts  
❌忽略 cultural sensitivities in documentation

## 3.3 Illustration Approach

### Style: Minimal Editorial Illustration
- **Use Case:** Section dividers, empty states, feature article accents
- **Style:** Simple line illustrations with selective color fills
- **Colors:** Limited to brand palette (Onion Purple, Sean Green, Orange, Neutral grays)
- **Subject:** Plateau landmarks, cultural symbols, abstract geometric patterns
- **Complexity:** Minimal — suggest rather than depict

### When to Use Illustrations

- Empty states and error pages
- Data visualization accents

### When NOT to Use Illustrations

- Official government content (maintain formality)

# 4. BRAND VOICE & TONE

## 4.1 Voice Characteristics

### 1. Authoritative
- **Definition:** We speak with confidence and expertise about Plateau State
- **Expression:** Well-researched, fact-based, credible
- **Avoid:** Vague claims, unsubstantiated opinions, hyperbole
- **Example:** "Jos, the capital of Plateau State, sits at 1,285 metres above sea level — the highest urban area in West Africa."

### 2. Warm
- **Definition:** We connect with readers on a human level
- **Expression:** Approachable, empathetic, community-focused
- **Avoid:** Cold institutional language, distant formality
- **Example:** "Behind every thriving business in Plateau is a story of resilience, community, and the unshakeable spirit of the Plateau people."

### 3. Proud
- **Definition:** We celebrate achievements without arrogance
- **Expression:** Uplifting, positive, celebratory
- **Avoid:** Bragging, dismissive tone toward others, insensitivity
- **Example:** "From the Jos Museum to the Shere Hills, Plateau State's heritage is a tapestry woven with centuries of culture and natural wonder."

### 4. Inclusive
- **Definition:** We represent all of Plateau's diverse communities
- **Expression:** Respectful, representative, multilingual-aware
- **Avoid:** Exclusionary language, stereotypes, cultural appropriation
- **Example:** "Whether you speak Berom, Hausa, Igbo, or English, Plateau is home — and BLOSSOM is your magazine."

---

## 4.2 Tone Variations by Section

### News & Current Affairs
- **Tone:** Objective, factual, timely
- **Headline Style:** Direct, informative, keyword-rich
- **Body:** Neutral, attribution-heavy, concise
- **Example Headline:** "Plateau State Government Approves ₦50 Billion Infrastructure Fund for 2026"

### Culture & Heritage
- **Tone:** Celebratory, respectful, storytelling
- **Headline Style:** Evocative, emotional, curiosity-driven
- **Body:** Descriptive, contextual, appreciative
- **Example Headline:** "The Berom People's Nzem Berom Festival: Where Drums Speak the Language of Ancestors"

### Business & Economy
- **Tone:** Professional, insightful, forward-looking
- **Headline Style:** Authoritative, data-informed, benefit-focused
- **Body:** Analytical, evidence-based, solution-oriented
- **Example Headline:** "How Plateau's Tech Startups Are attracting ₦2.3 Billion in Venture Capital"

### Tourism & Lifestyle
- **Tone:** Aspirational, inviting, experiential
- **Headline Style:** Sensory, action-oriented, visually evocative
- **Body:** Descriptive, recommendation-rich, personal
- **Example Headline:** "7 Hidden Waterfalls in Plateau That Will Take Your Breath Away"

### Government & Policy
- **Tone:** Formal, informative, balanced
- **Headline Style:** Clear, factual, non-partisan
- **Body:** Structured, attribution-heavy, neutral
- **Example Headline:** "Understanding the New Plateau State Digital Infrastructure Policy"

---

## 4.3 Writing Guidelines

### Headlines
- **Length:** 8–12 words maximum
- **Style:** Title Case (AP Style)
- **Voice:** Active voice preferred
- **Numbers:** Spell out one through nine; use numerals for 10+
- **Punctuation:** No periods at end of headlines

### Subheadlines
- **Length:** 12–20 words
- **Style:** Sentence case
- **Voice:** Active or passive (context-dependent)
- **Function:** Expand on headline, provide context

### Body Copy
- **Paragraph Length:** 3–5 sentences maximum
- **Sentence Length:** 15–25 words average
- **Voice:** Active voice (80% minimum)
- **Contractions:** Permitted in feature content; avoided in hard news
- **Numbers:** Spell out one through nine; numerals for 10+ (except percentages: use %)

### Call-to-Action (CTA)
- **Style:** Verb-first, action-oriented
- **Length:** 2–5 words
- **Voice:** Direct, inviting
- **Examples:** "Read the Full Story", "Explore Plateau", "Subscribe Now", "Share This Article"

### Use Light Mode When:
- Default user experience (new visitors)

### Use Dark Mode When:
- User preference (toggle in settings)
- Night/evening reading
- Long-form reading content (reduces eye strain)
- Video content (better contrast)
